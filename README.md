# SmartRun
Voice guided running / training plans.
Powered by Ajie.AI

SmartRun is a GPS-powered running app designed to make your workouts smarter and more fun.  
Track your runs, follow guided training plans, and stay motivated with real-time coaching.

## Features
- 🗺️ GPS tracking with live map
- ⏱️ Distance, time, and pace monitoring
- 🎯 Multiple workout modes:
  - 👟 Easy Run (30 min target)
  - 👟 Interval Training (Stamina goal)
  - 👟 Distance Target (Voice-guided run/walk)
- 🏆 Achievements & history to track progress

## How to Use
1. Open the app and connect GPS.
2. Choose your workout mode.
3. Tap **Start** and begin your run.
4. Follow the coach and enjoy your training!

---
💡 Built for runners who want a simple, motivating, and effective way to train.
