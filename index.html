Privacy Policy for SmartRUN

Effective date: [01/08/2026]

SmartRUN ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal running and training app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using SmartRUN, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Account information

If you create an account (by email/password or by signing in with Google), we collect your email address and display name. This is used to identify your account and let you sign in on multiple devices.

1.2 Location data (GPS)

SmartRun uses your device's GPS to record the route, distance, and pace of your runs while a run is in progress, including while the app is in the background so tracking continues if your screen is off or you switch apps mid-run. Location data is only collected during an active tracked run - the app does not track your location at any other time.

1.3 Run history and fitness data

We store the running data you generate while using the app, including:

  • Distance, duration, pace, and route (GPS coordinates) for each run
  • Workout type (interval training, distance target, etc.)
  • Optional photos you attach to a run ("selfies")
  • Your weight and stamina goal, if you choose to enter them (used only to estimate calories burned)

1.4 Diagnostic and crash data

We use Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash reports and basic device/app diagnostic information (device model, OS version, app version, and technical logs) when the app crashes or encounters an error. This helps us find and fix bugs. This data is not linked to your identity beyond an internal device/installation identifier.

1.5 Third-party integrations you choose to connect

ServiceDirectionWhat's shared
Strava Import If you connect your Strava account, we read your past running activities (distance, time, route, pace) from Strava and copy them into your SmartRun history. We do not post anything to your Strava account.
Google Health / Health Connect Export If you enable this, we write your completed SmartRun runs (distance, duration, workout type) to Android Health Connect, so other health apps you use can see them. We do not read any data from Health Connect.
Google Sign-In Authentication Used only to verify your identity and create/access your account. We receive your name, email, and profile photo URL from Google; we do not access your other Google data.

Each of these is entirely optional and only active if you explicitly connect it in Profile settings.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the app's core function: tracking, recording, and displaying your runs
  • To back up your run history to the cloud (Firebase/Firestore) so it's available if you switch or reinstall on a new device
  • To let you share a summary image of your run with others, if you choose to
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and bugs
  • To provide optional Pro features, if you purchase or are in a free trial of SmartRun Pro

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Where your data is stored

Your run history is stored locally on your device, and backed up to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) if you're signed in. Firebase stores data on Google Cloud infrastructure. Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Delete a run: You can delete any individual run from your history at any time.
  • Delete your account: You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud and local data at any time from Profile > Delete Account. This action is irreversible.
  • Disconnect integrations: You can disconnect Strava or Google Health/Health Connect at any time from Profile > Connect Partner Apps.
  • Location permission: You can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings, though this will prevent the app's core run-tracking feature from working.

5. Data retention

We retain your account and run history data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is deleted from our cloud storage; locally stored data on your device is deleted at the same time. Crash/diagnostic data may be retained separately for a limited period for debugging purposes.

6. Children's privacy

SmartRun is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Effective date" above and, where appropriate, notify you within the app.
